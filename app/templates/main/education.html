Waste Management Education

Better sorting, stronger recovery, cleaner communities.

A strong circular economy starts with everyday waste decisions. Here are practical guidelines for households, businesses, and collection teams.

Step 01

Sort at source

Separate plastic, paper, metal, glass, and organic waste early to improve collection quality and recycling value.

Step 02

Keep materials clean

Rinse containers and keep recyclable materials dry where possible to reduce contamination and processing costs.

Step 03

Work with trusted partners

Use verified collectors and recycling agencies to improve traceability, reliability, and environmental outcomes.
