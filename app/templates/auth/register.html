Registration guide

How to register successfully.

Use this quick guide to complete your EcoBridge Ghana profile correctly the first time.

  1. 01

    Choose your registration type

    Select whether you are registering as a collector or a recycling agency.

  2. 02

    Enter working contact details

    Use the phone number and email address partners should use to reach you directly.

  3. 03

    Add your service location

    Fill in your region, city, and area so people can find you by location in the directory.

  4. 04

    Select waste types and describe your work

    Choose all waste streams you handle and write a short summary of your service or recycling focus.

  5. 05

    Create the account and check your email

    After submitting the form, your profile goes live immediately and a confirmation email is sent to you.

Helpful tip

A logo or profile photo is optional, but it helps your listing look more trusted and easier to recognize.
